## The Board's terms

What this plugin's builders noted, to check against the Board's own words,
which can change. Not legal advice.

- The Board's site says its information is public domain and may be copied
  and distributed without permission, and asks to be cited as the source
  ([disclaimer](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm)). US
  government work: the datasets declare it kept, derived use not
  restricted, displayed.
- H.10's rates are noon buying rates in New York certified by the Federal
  Reserve Bank of New York for customs purposes
  ([about H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/about.htm)):
  midday rates, released weekly, not a market's close.
- H.15 reprints series from others: the federal funds effective rate is the
  New York Fed's, and the Treasury yields are Treasury's
  ([about H.15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/)).
- The zero curve is a staff research product, "not an official Federal
  Reserve statistical release", subject to revision without notice
  ([the nominal yield curve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm);
  Gurkaynak, Sack and Wright, FEDS 2006-28).
- The plugin reads the Board directly, not FRED, whose API has terms and a
  key of its own.

## Attribution

Federal Reserve System". The Datasets page shows the same.

records; neither the plugin nor this page says whether your use meets
anyone's terms. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
Federal Reserve.
